Cutting a caption until an editor accepts it is an awkward way to write. **Counting characters helps you work toward a length target before publishing**, but it does not decide which words deserve to stay.

[Antrapost’s free caption counter](https://www.antrapost.com/en/tools/caption-character-counter?utm_source=ulises_io&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=antrapost_guides&utm_content=en_contador-caracteres-captions-instagram) shows characters, words and hashtags as you type. Antrapost is one of my projects; the counter needs no registration and does not send your draft to a server to calculate its results.

## What the tool counts

The panel displays four different measurements:

| Measurement | Meaning | Possible use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Characters | Includes spaces and line breaks | Compare the draft’s total length |
| Without spaces or line breaks | Excludes whitespace characters | Understand the remaining content’s length |
| Words | Estimate based on spaces and line breaks | Compare longer and shorter versions |
| Hashtags | Matches the counter’s hashtag pattern | See how much of your text consists of tags |

Repeated hashtags are counted separately. This is not a list of unique tags or a recommendation about how many you should use.

## An example you can verify

Paste `Hola mundo #Python`. The tool shows **18 characters, 16 excluding spaces, 3 words and 1 hashtag**. This small example distinguishes the measurements; it is not an editorial target.

Now add a line break, another tag or an emoji and see what changes. You will understand why “the number of letters” does not always explain a post’s actual length.

A combined emoji can contain several Unicode code points. This counter counts code points, so something displayed as one symbol may add more than one character. The result can differ from a social platform’s editor.

## Prepare a caption step by step

1. Write or paste your draft under **Post text**.
2. Read the message first; do not start deleting words to improve a number.
3. Choose your target under **Your character target**.
4. Check how much room remains or how far you exceed the target.
5. Remove repetition while retaining context, conditions and the important action.
6. Copy the text and check it in the destination platform’s editor.

The target you enter is personal. It does not automatically represent Instagram, Facebook or Threads limits, and the tool does not certify that a post will be accepted.

## Shorten without losing the message

Look for phrases that announce what follows: “In this post, I would like to share…” can often disappear. Keep the information that helps readers decide rather than a generic introduction.

For example, “I want to let you know that a new application update with improvements is now available” can become “The app now exports your expenses.” The second statement is more concrete, provided that feature exists.

If an important condition matters — a date, limitation or eligible audience — do not remove it merely to reach a pleasant length. Accuracy is part of good writing too.

When presenting a project, explain the problem first and then an action to try it. This [side-project launch guide](/en/launch-side-project-social-media-content-plan) connects the caption to the rest of the launch.

## Hashtag and word counts are not a complete strategy

The number of hashtags does not predict reach. Use tags that actually describe the content and check for mistakes or accidental repetition.

There is no universal word count that makes every post work either. A technical explanation may need more context than an update announcement. Use the counter to compare drafts rather than replace editorial judgment.

## Save the text before leaving

Drafts are processed in the browser and are not saved after reloading or closing. Use **Copy text** and retain a version in your notes.

Then inspect links, account names and files. Counting characters does not review those elements: the [pre-publishing checklist](/en/social-media-publishing-checklist-ai-review-prompts) organizes a more complete manual review.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the default target of 500 a platform limit?

No. It is an editable draft target. Consult the destination’s requirements and editor before sending.

### Do spaces and line breaks count?

Yes, in the total. The counter also offers a measurement excluding them so you can distinguish both.

### Does the tool judge whether my post is good?

No. It measures the text using the described rules. You review clarity, accuracy, relevance and the final result on each network.
