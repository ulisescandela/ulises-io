## Decide where the image will appear first

The editor offers three presets and custom dimensions. These presets are design starting points, not guarantees of compatibility with every publishing format on a platform.

| Editor preset | Dimensions | Example use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square | 1080 × 1080 | A photo or card with a short message |
| Portrait | 1080 × 1350 | An image with more room for content |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 | A full-screen vertical composition |
| Custom | Between 64 and 4096 per side | An output with specific requirements |

Before exporting, check what your destination supports and how its previews display the image. The same image can appear complete when opened and cropped elsewhere in the interface.

## Crop or keep the whole image

**Crop** fills the canvas. When aspect ratios differ, part of the image falls outside it. Horizontal and vertical controls let you move the crop to choose what to keep.

**Fit full image** keeps the entire photo within the canvas. When space remains, the editor adds a background in your chosen color. This helps with screenshots containing buttons, charts with labels or illustrations where you need every detail.

For example, a horizontal dashboard screenshot does not naturally fit a portrait design. Keep it complete with a background and accept that it looks smaller, or crop an important detail. You do not need to show the entire application in one image; a second screenshot can show another area.

## Common resizing mistakes

- **Enlarging a small image and expecting more detail.** More output pixels do not recover information missing from the original.
- **Cropping essential text or interface elements.** Inspect all four corners and the center, not just the subject of a photo.
- **Choosing a format out of habit.** A wide screenshot and a portrait need different compositions.
- **Confusing dimensions with file size.** A 1080-pixel image can still be too large in bytes. For that problem, use the [image compressor](/en/compress-jpg-webp-images-for-free).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to upload my photo to a server?

The tool processes the image in your browser. It requires no account and does not save the photo for another session. Download the result before closing or reloading.

### Can I resize without cutting anything off?

Yes: choose **Fit full image**. You will need a background when the photo and canvas have different aspect ratios.

### Does changing dimensions improve sharpness?

Not necessarily. Reducing an image can make it more suitable for its use; enlarging it creates no new detail and can reveal insufficient resolution.
