## Which format should you choose?

No format wins in every situation. Choose according to the content and destination, then compare the resulting file.

| Format | Transparency | One reason to choose it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JPG | No | Photos needing an opaque output supported by the destination |
| PNG | Yes | Graphics, screenshots or images where transparency matters |
| WebP | Yes | An output that may balance size and quality when supported |

PNG does not automatically mean “better photo,” and WebP does not automatically mean “smaller file.” Quality, dimensions and content also influence the result.

## Converting JPG to PNG does not remove a background

This is a common misunderstanding: JPG has no transparency, and saving it as PNG does not decide which pixels to remove. You get a PNG containing the photograph, including its background.

Background removal requires another process. The converter preserves transparency when it already exists and the output format supports it; it does not automatically cut out people, products or logos.

Imagine a photograph of a shoe on a white table. Converting it to PNG keeps the white table. An image of the shoe with a transparent background needs that background removed beforehand.
